
VEHICLE INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Name: Comprehensive Vehicle Insurance
Policy Type: Comprehensive

1. POLICY COVERAGE

This policy covers accidental damage to the insured vehicle,
including collision, overturning and external accidental damage.

2. THIRD PARTY COVERAGE

The policy covers legal liability towards third parties
for accidental injury or property damage, subject to policy terms.

3. POLICY PERIOD

Coverage is valid only between the policy start date
and policy expiry date.

4. DEDUCTIBLE

A compulsory deductible of Rs. 5000 applies to
each eligible own-damage claim.

5. EXCLUSIONS

The policy does not cover:
- Normal wear and tear.
- Mechanical or electrical breakdown without an insured event.
- Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol.
- Accidents occurring outside the policy period.

6. CLAIM REQUIREMENTS

The policyholder must:
- Notify the insurance company about the accident.
- Submit the claim form.
- Provide vehicle registration and driving licence details.
- Submit repair estimates and supporting documents.

7. CLAIM SETTLEMENT

The payable claim amount is calculated based on
admissible repair costs after deducting the applicable
deductible, depreciation and other policy adjustments.
